Visit statistics (GoatCounter)

I use GoatCounter to see how many people visit the site and which pages or links (CV download, project links) they use. GoatCounter:

  • does not use cookies or store anything in your browser;
  • does not store your IP address or full browser identifier — only aggregated counts such as page, referring site, browser and system name, screen width, language and country (derived from the IP and then discarded);
  • keeps a temporary, in-memory link between IP, browser and site for at most 8 hours, only to avoid counting the same visit twice;
  • stores data on servers in Finland and Germany (EU) and does not share it with third parties.

Legal basis: my legitimate interest in knowing, in aggregate, how the site is used (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Details: GoatCounter privacy policy. Blocking scripts or using an ad-blocker simply means you are not counted.

Hosting (GitHub Pages)

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address when you load a page and, according to GitHub, logs it for security purposes. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub privacy statement.

Preferences in your browser

If you switch theme (dark/light) or language (EN/IT), that choice is saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it. It never leaves your device and you can clear it at any time from your browser settings.

Fonts and other resources

All fonts and scripts are served from this site; nothing is loaded from Google or other CDNs. The interactive project dashboards linked from the portfolio follow the same approach; the marine litter dashboard additionally loads map tiles from Esri, which is needed to display the map.

External links

Links to LinkedIn, GitHub or other sites take you to services with their own privacy policies.

Statistiche di visita (GoatCounter)

Uso GoatCounter per sapere quante persone visitano il sito e quali pagine o link usano (download del CV, link ai progetti). GoatCounter:

  • non usa cookie e non salva nulla nel tuo browser;
  • non conserva il tuo indirizzo IP né l'identificativo completo del browser: solo conteggi aggregati come pagina, sito di provenienza, nome di browser e sistema, larghezza dello schermo, lingua e paese (ricavato dall'IP e poi scartato);
  • mantiene in memoria, per al massimo 8 ore, un collegamento temporaneo tra IP, browser e sito, solo per non contare due volte la stessa visita;
  • conserva i dati su server in Finlandia e Germania (UE) e non li condivide con terzi.

Base giuridica: il mio legittimo interesse a sapere, in forma aggregata, come viene usato il sito (art. 6, par. 1, lett. f GDPR). Dettagli: informativa di GoatCounter. Se blocchi gli script o usi un ad-blocker, semplicemente non vieni contato.
