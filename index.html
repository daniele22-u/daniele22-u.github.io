About

I'm a biomedical engineer working where signals meet learning. I did my bachelor's at the University of Cagliari and I'm finishing an MSc in Biomedical Engineering at Politecnico di Milano, on the bioinformatics track.

Most of my work starts from a noisy physiological recording — an EEG, a vital-sign stream, a pressure platform, an MRI volume — and ends with a model that has to make sense of it. I like owning the whole chain: defining the problem, cleaning the data, building and validating the model, and the moment a pipeline finally tells you something real.

Right now I'm writing my master's thesis on deep learning for EEG decoding. Outside the lab I've spent four years as an elected student representative, first in Cagliari and then at PoliMi, because I care about how universities work for the people inside them.
