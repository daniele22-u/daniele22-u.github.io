About

I'm a biomedical engineer working where signals meet learning. I did my bachelor's at the University of Cagliari and I'm now finishing an MSc in Information Bioengineering at Politecnico di Milano.

Most of my work starts from a noisy physiological recording — an EEG, a pressure platform, an MRI volume — and ends with a model that has to make sense of it. I like the whole chain: acquisition, cleaning, the maths in between, and the moment a pipeline finally tells you something real.

Right now I'm writing my master's thesis on decoding imagined speech from brain activity with graph neural networks. Outside the lab I've spent years as a student representative, first in Cagliari and then at PoliMi, because I care about how universities work for the people inside them.

● RECCH-01 // MSC THESIS

Python · PyTorch · PyG · MNE · 2025 – ongoing

Imagined speech decoding with graph & hypergraph neural networks

Master's thesis at Politecnico di Milano (DEIB). Can we tell which word someone is silently thinking from their EEG? I model each recording as a graph of electrodes and train graph and hypergraph neural networks end-to-end on the raw signal, with the long-term goal of a semantic “neural dictionary”.
