About

I'm a biomedical engineer working where signals meet learning. I did my bachelor's at the University of Cagliari and I'm finishing an MSc in Biomedical Engineering at Politecnico di Milano, on the bioinformatics track.

Most of my work starts from a noisy physiological recording — an EEG, a vital-sign stream, a pressure platform, an MRI volume — and ends with a model that has to make sense of it. I like owning the whole chain: defining the problem, cleaning the data, building and validating the model, and the moment a pipeline finally tells you something real.

Right now I'm writing my master's thesis on deep learning for EEG decoding. Outside the lab I've been an elected student representative since 2023, first in Cagliari and then at PoliMi, because I care about how universities work for the people inside them.

Imagined speech decoding with graph & hypergraph neural networks

Master's thesis at Politecnico di Milano. Can we tell which word someone is silently thinking from their EEG? I design deep learning models that treat each recording as a graph of electrodes and learn end-to-end from the raw signal, with the long-term goal of a semantic “neural dictionary”.
